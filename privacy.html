「出岛」隐私政策

政策版本：2026-10-06

生效日期：2026-10-06

最近更新：2026-10-24

我们知道，刚到一个新国家，把自己的联系方式交给别人需要信任。这份政策用尽量简单的话，说明「出岛」收集什么信息、为什么收集、给谁看、存多久，以及你可以怎么管理自己的信息。

1. 我们是谁

「出岛」App 及相关的安顿咨询和陪同服务，由 Zibo Gong 以个人身份经营，下文称"我们"。我们位于澳洲昆士兰州布里斯班。

联系我们：drfellingood@gmail.com

2. 我们收集哪些信息

(1) 你提交预约申请时填写的信息

当你在 App 里提交预约申请，我们会收到：

  • 称呼：你的名字或昵称。用昵称也可以。
  • 联系方式：你填写的微信号、邮箱或电话。
  • 期望日期：你希望咨询的日期（布里斯班时间）。这只是意向，不代表已经安排好。
  • 问题描述：你想解决的问题，以及你自己选择写下的情况。
  • 你选择的服务项目、提交时间、你同意的隐私政策版本，以及申请状态（等待确认 / 已确认 / 无法接单 / 已取消）和向导给你的回复。

提交前，你需要勾选确认"我已年满 18 岁"，以及勾选同意本政策（见第 4 条）。

请不要在申请里填写护照号、签证号、银行卡号、密码，或健康状况等敏感信息。我们不需要这些信息来安排咨询。

(2) 只保存在你手机上、不会上传的信息

  • 清单进度：你在"准备出发 / 刚刚抵达 / 慢慢安顿"清单里勾选了哪些事项，以及你选择的阶段。
  • 未提交的草稿：你填写到一半、还没有提交的申请内容。

这些信息只存在你的设备上，我们看不到。删除 App 或清除 App 数据后，这些信息会被移除。

(3) 匿名账号 ID

你不需要注册，也不需要输入密码。第一次提交申请时，App 会通过我们的服务器服务商 Supabase 的"匿名登录"功能，自动生成一个随机的账号 ID，和你的申请一起保存在服务器上。

  • 它只用来让你这台手机读到你自己提交的申请，不用于其他目的。
  • 它不是你的手机序列号，也不是广告标识符，不包含你的姓名或联系方式。
  • 这个 ID 只保存在当前这台手机的 App 里。换手机、删除并重装 App，或清除 App 数据后，你在 App 里就看不到以前的申请了。这些申请仍按第 6 条的期限保存在服务器上；如需查看、取消或删除，请联系我们（见第 7 条）。

(4) 服务器技术信息

和大多数网络服务一样，我们的服务器服务商可能会为了安全和故障排查，短期记录访问时间和 IP 地址等技术信息。这些记录的保存期限以服务商的设置为准，我们不会用它们识别你的身份。

(5) App 以外的信息

  • 沟通记录：我们通过你留下的联系方式和你沟通（例如微信、邮件或电话），沟通中你告诉我们的信息。
  • 付款记录：App 内不能付款。如果你通过银行转账等方式在 App 外付款，我们会保存付款人姓名、金额、日期等记录，用于对账和报税。

我们不收集的信息：App 不使用统计分析工具、不投放广告、不使用第三方追踪；不读取你的通讯录、照片、位置或广告标识符。

3. 我们怎样使用这些信息

我们只为以下目的使用你的信息：

  • 查看你的预约申请，判断能否提供帮助，联系你确认，或告诉你无法接单；
  • 安排和提供你选择的服务，以及服务后的跟进；
  • 在有新申请时通知我们（见第 4 条中的 Resend）；
  • 处理付款、退款和必要的记账、报税；
  • 回答你的问题、处理你的投诉或隐私请求；
  • 保障服务安全，防止滥用；
  • 遵守法律要求。

我们不会出售你的个人信息，不会用它投放广告，也不会把它提供给数据经纪商。如果我们以后想向你发送推广信息，会先单独征得你的同意。

4. 谁能看到你的信息

  • 负责你这项服务的向导：你的预约申请会发给负责该项服务的向导。目前所有服务都由创始人本人（向导 George）提供。将来经我们面试审核的其他向导加入后，你的申请只会发给负责你所选服务的向导，App 里会显示向导的名字。向导须遵守我们对保密和数据使用的要求。
  • 我们：创始人通过只供向导使用的「向导工作台」网页查看和处理申请（确认、告诉你无法接单、填写回复）。登录需要密码和两步验证，普通用户无法进入。
  • 为我们提供技术服务的第三方：
    • Supabase（服务器、数据库和匿名登录）：保存你提交的申请和匿名账号 ID。
    • Resend（邮件发送）：有新申请时，我们用 Resend 给创始人发一封通知邮件。邮件只包含服务名称和期望日期，不包含你的称呼、联系方式或问题全文。这封邮件也会保存在创始人使用的邮箱服务里。
    这些服务商只按我们的指示处理数据，不能把你的信息用于它们自己的目的。
  • 你选择的沟通工具：如果你留下微信号，我们会通过微信联系你，对话内容由微信按其自身的隐私政策处理。
  • 法律要求时：在法律要求或允许的情况下（例如执法机关依法要求），我们可能需要提供信息。

提交申请前，App 会请你勾选：

我同意按《隐私政策》把以上信息发送给「出岛」和负责这项服务的向导，用于联系我安排服务

不勾选就不能提交。

5. 信息存放在哪里，是否会传到境外

  • 你提交的申请保存在 Supabase 位于澳洲悉尼（ap-southeast-2 区域）的服务器上。
  • Supabase 是境外公司，它的支持团队或下级服务商可能在维护和排查故障时从澳洲以外访问数据。
  • 新申请的通知邮件经由 Resend 发送，Resend 把邮件内容存放在美国。邮件只含服务名称和期望日期。
  • 我们和向导在出行时，也可能在境外查看申请。
  • 如果你使用微信等境外通讯工具和我们联系，相关信息会由该服务商按其规定存放。

我们会选择有合同保护和安全承诺的服务商。

6. 保存多久

  • 服务已完成的申请：服务结束后 12 个月 删除联系方式和申请内容。
  • 无法接单或已取消的申请：90 天 后删除。
  • 一直没有被确认的申请：在期望日期过后 90 天 删除。
  • 匿名账号 ID：你可以在 App 里随时删除全部数据，删除后匿名账号会一并删除。如果你没有删除，在你名下已没有任何申请、并且 90 天没有使用 App 后，匿名账号会被自动删除。
  • 付款和记账记录：按澳洲税务法规要求的期限保存，期满后删除。
  • 只在你手机上的数据：由你自己控制，删除 App 或清除 App 数据即被移除。

如果法律要求我们保存更久，或为了处理尚未解决的争议，我们会保存到必要的时间为止。

7. 你的权利，以及怎样申请

在 App 里就能做的

  • 取消申请：在"等待确认"时可以随时取消；"已确认"的申请须在约定时间 24 小时前 取消。取消后，向导不会再就这份申请联系你，信息按第 6 条在 90 天后删除。
  • 删除全部数据：在「我的」页面选择"删除我的全部数据"，会删除这台手机在服务器上的所有申请和匿名账号 ID，并清除本机记录。付款记录因税务要求需单独保留。

通过联系我们可以做的

  • 查阅：询问我们保存了你的哪些信息，并获得一份副本；
  • 更正：要求更正不准确或已过时的信息；
  • 删除：要求删除你的全部信息（法律要求保留的记录除外，例如付款记录）。如果你换了手机或重装了 App、在 App 里看不到以前的申请，请用这个方式；
  • 撤回同意：随时撤回你对我们使用你信息的同意。撤回后，我们可能无法继续为你提供服务；
  • 使用化名：你可以用昵称提交申请。

怎样联系：发邮件到 drfellingood@gmail.com，写明你提交申请时用的称呼、联系方式和大约的提交日期，方便我们找到你的记录。为保护你的信息，我们可能需要核实你的身份（例如通过你留下的联系方式确认）。我们一般会在 30 天内 回复。这些请求不收费。

8. 只面向 18 岁及以上

本服务只面向 18 岁及以上 的用户。提交申请时，你需要勾选确认"我已年满 18 岁"。如果你未满 18 岁，请不要提交申请。如果我们发现收到了未满 18 岁的人提交的申请，会删除相关信息。如果你是家长或监护人，认为孩子向我们提交了信息，请联系我们。

9. 我们怎样保护你的信息

我们会采取合理的措施保护你的信息，包括：

  • App 和服务器之间的数据通过加密连接（HTTPS）传输；
  • 设置访问权限：你的手机只能读到用你的匿名账号 ID 提交的申请；你的申请只有「出岛」的管理员和负责这项服务的向导可以查看；管理员进入后台需要通过两步验证；
  • 通知邮件不包含你的联系方式和问题全文；
  • 只收集安排服务所需的信息，并按第 6 条按时删除；
  • 选择有安全承诺的技术服务商。

没有任何网络传输或存储方式能做到绝对安全，我们无法保证信息永远不会被未经授权地访问。如果发生可能对你造成严重伤害的数据泄露，我们会尽快通知你，并告诉你可以采取的措施。

10. 隐私政策的变更

我们可能会更新这份政策，例如增加新的功能或服务商时。重要变化会在 App 里提示，并更新顶部的"最近更新"日期。如果变化涉及以新的方式使用你已经提交的信息，我们会先征得你的同意。

11. 联系我们和投诉

如果你对我们处理个人信息的方式有疑问或投诉，请先联系：drfellingood@gmail.com。我们会在 30 天内回复。

如果你对我们的处理结果不满意，可以向澳洲信息专员办公室（OAIC）咨询：www.oaic.gov.au（电话 1300 363 992）。

Arrivo Privacy Policy

Policy version: 2026-10-06

Effective date: 2026-10-06

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Sharing your contact details with someone when you have just arrived in a new country takes trust. This policy explains, in plain language, what information Arrivo (出岛) collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how you can manage it.

1. Who we are

The Arrivo app and the related settling-in consultation and accompaniment services are operated by Zibo Gong as an individual ("we", "us"), based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia.

Contact: drfellingood@gmail.com

2. What we collect

(1) Information you provide when you submit a booking request

  • Name: your name or a nickname. A nickname is fine.
  • Contact details: the WeChat ID, email address or phone number you enter.
  • Preferred date: the date you would like the consultation (Brisbane time). This is a preference only, not a confirmed arrangement.
  • Description of your question: what you would like help with, and any background you choose to share.
  • The service you selected, the time of submission, the privacy policy version you agreed to, the request status (Waiting for confirmation / Confirmed / Unable to take / Cancelled) and the guide's reply.

Before submitting, you must tick to confirm "I am 18 or older" and tick to agree to this policy (see section 4).

Please do not include passport or visa numbers, bank card numbers, passwords, or health information in your request. We do not need them to arrange a consultation.

(2) Information stored only on your device (never uploaded)

  • Checklist progress: which items you have ticked and which stage you selected.
  • Unsubmitted drafts: request details you have started but not submitted.

This information stays on your device and we cannot see it. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

(3) Anonymous account ID

You do not need to sign up or enter a password. The first time you submit a request, the app uses the "anonymous sign-in" feature of our hosting provider, Supabase, to create a random account ID, which is stored with your request on our server.

  • It is used only so that your phone can show the requests you submitted, and for nothing else.
  • It is not your device serial number or advertising identifier, and it does not contain your name or contact details.
  • The ID is kept only in the app on your current phone. If you change phones, delete and reinstall the app, or clear its data, you will no longer see your earlier requests in the app. Those requests remain on our server for the periods in section 6; contact us (section 7) to access, cancel or delete them.

(4) Server technical information

Our hosting provider may briefly record technical information such as access times and IP addresses for security and troubleshooting. How long these records are kept depends on the provider's settings; we do not use them to identify you.

(5) Information outside the app

  • Communications: information you share when we contact you through the details you provided (e.g. WeChat, email or phone).
  • Payment records: payments cannot be made in the app. If you pay outside the app (e.g. bank transfer), we keep the payer name, amount and date for reconciliation and tax purposes.

What we do not collect: the app uses no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking; it does not access your contacts, photos, location or advertising identifier.

3. How we use your information

Only to:

  • review your booking request and contact you to confirm it, or tell you we are unable to take it;
  • arrange and provide the service you chose, and follow up afterwards;
  • notify us when a new request arrives (see "Resend" in section 4);
  • handle payments, refunds, bookkeeping and tax obligations;
  • respond to your questions, complaints and privacy requests;
  • keep the service secure and prevent misuse;
  • comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. If we ever wish to send you promotional messages, we will ask for your separate consent first.

4. Who can see your information

  • The guide responsible for your service: your request is sent to the guide who provides the service you selected. Currently all services are provided by the founder (guide George). If other guides, interviewed and approved by us, join later, your request will only go to the guide for the service you chose, whose name is shown in the app. Guides must follow our confidentiality and data-use requirements.
  • Us: the founder reviews and handles requests (confirming them, telling you we are unable to take them, and adding replies) through a separate guide-only web page (the guide workbench). Signing in requires a password and two-step verification; ordinary users cannot access it.
  • Third-party technology providers:
    • Supabase (server, database and anonymous sign-in): stores your submitted requests and anonymous account ID.
    • Resend (email delivery): when a new request arrives, we use Resend to send the founder a notification email. The email contains only the service name and preferred date, not your name, contact details or full question. The email is also stored by the founder's own email provider.
    These providers process data only on our instructions and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Messaging tools you choose: if you give us a WeChat ID, we will contact you on WeChat, and those messages are handled under WeChat's own privacy policy.
  • When required by law: where required or authorised by law (e.g. a lawful request from a law enforcement agency).

Before you submit a request, the app asks you to tick:

"I agree, under the Privacy Policy, to send the information above to Arrivo and the guide responsible for this service, so they can contact me to arrange the service."

You cannot submit without ticking it. (Translation of the in-app Chinese wording.)

5. Where your information is stored and overseas access

  • Submitted requests are stored on Supabase servers in Sydney, Australia (ap-southeast-2 region).
  • Supabase is an overseas company; its support staff or sub-processors may access data from outside Australia for maintenance and troubleshooting.
  • New-request notification emails are sent through Resend, which stores email content in the United States. These emails contain only the service name and preferred date.
  • Guides and we may view requests while travelling overseas.
  • If you contact us via an overseas messaging service such as WeChat, that information is stored according to that provider's terms.

We choose providers with contractual and security commitments.

6. How long we keep it

  • Requests for completed services: contact details and request content are deleted 12 months after the service ends.
  • Requests we were unable to take, or that were cancelled: deleted after 90 days.
  • Requests that were never confirmed: deleted 90 days after the preferred date.
  • Anonymous account ID: you can delete all your data in the app at any time, and the anonymous account is deleted with it. If you do not, it is deleted automatically once no requests remain under it and the app has not been used for 90 days.
  • Payment and bookkeeping records: kept for the period required by Australian tax law, then deleted.
  • On-device data: under your control; removed when you delete the app or clear its data.

We may keep information longer where the law requires it or to resolve an outstanding dispute.

7. Your rights and how to make a request

In the app

  • Cancel a request: while it is waiting for confirmation, at any time; once confirmed, at least 24 hours before the agreed time. After cancelling, the guide will not contact you about that request, and it is deleted after 90 days (section 6).
  • Delete all your data: in "Mine", choose "Delete all my data". This deletes all requests from this phone and its anonymous account ID from our server, and clears local data. Payment records must be kept separately for tax purposes.

By contacting us

  • access the information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or out-of-date information;
  • delete all your information (except records we must keep by law, such as payment records). Use this if you changed phones or reinstalled the app and can no longer see your requests;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (we may then be unable to provide the service);
  • use a nickname when submitting a request.

How: email drfellingood@gmail.com with the name and contact details you used and the approximate submission date. To protect your information, we may need to verify your identity (e.g. via the contact details you gave us). We usually respond within 30 days. There is no charge.

8. Users aged 18 and over only

This service is only for people aged 18 and over. When submitting a request you must tick to confirm "I am 18 or older". If you are under 18, please do not submit a request. If we learn we have received a request from someone under 18, we will delete it. Parents or guardians who believe their child has given us information can contact us.

9. How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our server;
  • access controls: your phone can only read requests submitted with its anonymous account ID; only Arrivo administrators and the responsible guide can view your request; administrators need two-step verification to enter the guide area;
  • notification emails exclude your contact details and full question;
  • collecting only what is needed to arrange the service, and deleting it on schedule (section 6);
  • choosing technology providers with security commitments.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed without authorisation. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you as soon as practicable and tell you what steps you can take.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features or providers. We will notify you in the app of significant changes and update the "Last updated" date. If a change involves using information you have already given us in a new way, we will ask for your consent first.

11. Contact and complaints

If you have a question or complaint about how we handle personal information, please contact drfellingood@gmail.com first. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): www.oaic.gov.au, phone 1300 363 992.
