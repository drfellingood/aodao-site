「出岛」帮助与支持

「出岛」帮助准备来澳、刚到布里斯班的中文新来者，一步步把安顿的事情理清楚。遇到问题，可以在这里找答案，或者直接联系我们。

「出岛」能做什么

  • 安顿清单：按"准备出发 / 刚刚抵达 / 慢慢安顿"三个阶段，列出住处、网络、交通、银行、找房、求职等常见事项，并附官方信息来源链接（例如 Study Australia、Translink）。清单进度只保存在你的手机上。
  • 向导咨询预约申请：选择一项服务（来澳安顿咨询、租房与选区咨询、求职起步咨询、本地陪同协助），填写称呼、联系方式、期望日期和你的问题，提交预约申请。向导会通过你留下的联系方式和你确认。
  • 目前只服务布里斯班，只面向 18 岁及以上 的用户。

常见问题

使用 App 需要注册吗？

不需要。不用注册账号，也不用输入密码。第一次提交申请时，App 会自动生成一个随机的匿名账号 ID，只用来让你的手机看到你自己的申请。

提交预约申请后，就安排好了吗？

还没有。提交的是"申请"，期望日期只是你的意向。在「我的」里可以看到申请状态：

  • 等待确认：向导还没有确认，会通过你留下的联系方式联系你；
  • 已确认：向导已确认，具体安排看向导回复；
  • 无法接单：向导这次接不了，你可以换个日期再提交一份新的申请；
  • 已取消：这份申请已取消。
可以取消申请吗？

可以。"等待确认"时可以随时在 App 里取消；"已确认"的申请须在约定时间 24 小时前 取消。目前还没有开始收费；付款后取消的退款规则，会在开始收费前公布在本页。

怎么付款？要多少钱？

App 里不能付款，提交申请也不产生费用。App 里显示的是参考价，服务内容和最终价格以向导联系你时确认的为准，确认后再付款。目前还没有开始收费；付款方式和退款规则会在开始收费前公布在本页。

我填的信息谁能看到？会被拿去做广告吗？

只有负责你所选服务的向导和我们能看到。我们不出售你的信息，不做广告，也没有第三方追踪。详见《隐私政策》。

怎样删除我的信息？

在「我的」页面选择"删除我的全部数据"，就能删除服务器上这台手机提交的所有申请和本机记录。也可以发邮件给我们，要求查阅、更正或删除你的信息。付款记录因税务要求需要保留一段时间。

换了手机，为什么看不到以前的申请了？

App 用一个只保存在当前手机上的匿名账号 ID 来读取你的申请。换手机、重装 App 或清除 App 数据后，这个 ID 就没有了，所以看不到以前的申请。申请本身还在，向导仍可以通过你留下的联系方式联系你；需要查看、取消或删除，请发邮件给我们。清单进度也只保存在原来的手机上，不会跟过来。

向导能帮我办签证、看房或开车接送吗？

不能。向导不提供签证、移民、法律或税务建议；租房只提供线上的一般信息和比较，不代看房、不代谈租金、不代交申请，也不经手租金或押金；不开车接送。这些事情请咨询注册移民代理、律师或持牌机构。

App 里的信息靠谱吗？

清单内容是一般信息，每一条都附了官方来源链接。政策和办理要求会变，请以官方网站和相关机构的最新信息为准。

CHUDAO Help & Support

CHUDAO (出岛) helps Chinese-speaking newcomers preparing to move to, or just arrived in, Brisbane get settled step by step.

What the app does

  • Settling-in checklists for three stages (before you leave / just arrived / settling in), with links to official sources (e.g. Study Australia, Translink). Checklist progress is stored only on your device.
  • Booking requests with a local guide: choose a service (settling-in consultation, housing and suburb consultation, job-search consultation, local accompaniment), enter your name, contact details, preferred date and question, and submit a request. The guide will contact you to confirm.
  • Currently available in Brisbane only, for people aged 18 and over.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No sign-up or password. The first time you submit a request, the app creates a random anonymous account ID, used only so your phone can show your own requests.

Is everything arranged once I submit?

Not yet. Your preferred date is only a preference. Statuses in "Mine": Waiting for confirmation (the guide will contact you), Confirmed (see the guide's reply), Unable to take (you can submit a new request for another date), Cancelled.

Can I cancel?

Yes. While waiting for confirmation, at any time in the app; once confirmed, at least 24 hours before the agreed time. We have not started charging yet; refund terms for cancelling after payment will be published on this page before we do.

How do I pay? How much is it?

No payments in the app, and submitting a request is free. Prices in the app are indicative; the final price is the one the guide confirms with you, and you pay after that. We have not started charging yet; payment methods and refund terms will be published on this page before we do.

Who can see my information? Is it used for ads?

Only the guide for your chosen service and us. We do not sell your information, show ads or use third-party tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my information?

In "Mine", choose "Delete all my data" to delete all requests from this phone on our server and clear local data. You can also email us to access, correct or delete your information. Payment records must be kept for a period for tax purposes.

Why can't I see my earlier requests on a new phone?

The app reads your requests with an anonymous account ID stored only on your current phone. Changing phones, reinstalling or clearing app data removes it. Your requests still exist and the guide can still reach you; email us to view, cancel or delete them. Checklist progress also stays on the old phone.

Can a guide help with visas, inspect properties or drive me around?

No. Guides do not give visa, migration, legal or tax advice; for housing they only provide general information and comparisons online (no inspections, rent negotiation, applications, or handling of rent or bonds); and they do not drive clients. Please consult a registered migration agent, a lawyer or a licensed professional for these matters.

Is the checklist information reliable?

It is general information with links to official sources. Rules change, so always check the official website or organisation.
